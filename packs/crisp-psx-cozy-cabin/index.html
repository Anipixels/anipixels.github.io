Crisp PSX Cozy Cabin: 63 cozy PS1-style low-poly 3D props

Crisp PSX Cozy Cabin is a cozy PS1-style 3D prop pack: a little log cabin you can furnish and build, its kitchen, a pile of cozy clutter and an autumn yard full of pumpkins. 63 low-poly props share one crunchy 512 px texture atlas. It is not horror: warm wood, patchwork quilts, gingham curtains, a crackling fire and a sleeping cat. Every prop comes as .glb, .fbx and .obj, with a .blend and a Godot 4 demo with a PSX shader.

What is included

  • Furniture (14) - bed, nightstand, table, chair, armchair, rocking chair, bookcase, wall shelf, wardrobe, wood stove, fireplace, kitchen counter, rug, table lamp
  • Kitchen (11) - pot, kettle, teapot, mug, plate stack, bread loaf, pie, jam jar, utensil crock, cutting board, apple basket

  • Cabin (8) - modular log cabin: log wall, window wall, door wall, gable, corner post, floor planks, roof slope, roof ridge

Details

  • 63 props in 5 categories, 11081 triangles for the whole pack. 59 of the 63 props are 400 triangles or fewer (median 168; the largest, a tree, is 528).
  • One 512 x 512 atlas of pixel textures (64 and 32 px cells, ordered dithering, warm palette), plus an emission atlas for the fire, flames, lamp shade, lantern and lit windows. One material for everything.
  • Soft baked shading in vertex colours, so props sit nicely even with flat or vertex lighting.
  • Modular cabin on a 2 m grid: log wall, window wall (with gingham curtains), door wall (with a heart in the door), gable, interlocking corner logs, plank floor, shingle roof and ridge. Textures line up across pieces.
  • Real-world metres, origins at the base, fronts facing the same way. Wall props (picture frame, wall clock, wall shelf, fireplace, flower box) have their back on the origin, so they snap straight onto a wall.
  • The fern fronds and leaf cards use alpha clip (cut-out, threshold 0.5).
  • The scene screenshots are Blender renders of the real files at 480 x 270 with 15-bit colour and dithering, shown 4x.
  • Download size: CrispPSXCozyCabin_Full.zip is 22.40 MB.

Formats and engines

  • Every prop as .glb (texture embedded), .fbx and .obj (with .mtl), with the texture atlas next to them.
  • A .blend file with every prop.
  • manifest.json listing every prop: category, description, triangle count, size in metres and file paths.
  • Godot 4 demo project: the furnished cabin and yard to orbit or walk around, day / night, plus a gallery of every prop, with a PSX shader (vertex snapping, affine texture warp, low-res render with 15-bit colour and dither) you can reuse. Load-tested headless in Godot 4.7.

Works with: Godot 4, Unity, Unreal, GameMaker 3D, three.js, Blender and anything else that loads glTF, FBX or OBJ. Use point / nearest texture filtering and no mipmaps for the crisp PSX look.

Questions

Which engines does it work with?

Any engine or tool that loads glTF, FBX or OBJ: Godot 4, Unity, Unreal, GameMaker 3D, three.js, Blender and others. The full pack includes a Godot 4 demo project.

Is it a horror pack?

No. It is cozy: warm wood, patchwork quilts, gingham curtains, a crackling fire, pumpkins and a sleeping cat.

How do I get the crisp PSX look?

Set texture filtering to nearest (point) and turn mipmaps off. The Godot 4 demo includes a PSX shader (vertex snapping, affine texture warp, low-res render with 15-bit colour and dither) that you can reuse in your own project.

Is there a browser demo?

No. The Godot 4 demo project is inside the full pack, and the screenshots on this page are Blender renders of the real files. The free sampler has 8 props to try in your engine first.

Is there a free version?

Yes: Crisp PSX Cozy Cabin Free has 8 props to test in your project.

Can I use it in a commercial game, and do I have to give credit?

It is royalty-free for commercial and non-commercial projects and no attribution is needed. You may not resell or redistribute the models or textures themselves, modified or not.

Was AI used to make it?
