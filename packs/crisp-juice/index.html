Crisp Juice: 149 VFX sprite sheets for casual and puzzle games

Crisp Juice is a pack of 149 casual-game visual effects as transparent PNG sprite sheets: gem bursts, match pops, line blasts, coins, stars, confetti, impacts, magic, highlights, text pops, elements, auras, emotes, trails and cozy ambience. Bright, chunky and easy to read at small sizes, with a sheets.json of frame data and a Godot 4 project with a ready-made scene for each effect.

Details

  • Version 2.0: 81 new effects (149 in total), a free update for existing owners. Everything that was in v1.0 is unchanged.
  • 149 sprite sheets in 12 folders. Frames run left to right, top to bottom, and every cell is square.
  • Cell sizes: 192 px (101 sheets), 256 px (30 sheets) and 128 px (18 sheets). 12 to 48 frames per effect, drawn for 30 frames per second.
  • Transparent PNG with straight (non-premultiplied) alpha.
  • 37 sheets are seamless loops (flagged in sheets.json); the other 112 play once.
  • 21 sheets (glows, sparkles, lightning, fireworks and similar) are marked "additive": true in sheets.json: they look best with additive blending on dark backgrounds and also work with normal blending.
  • The text pops are English words drawn as shapes (not a font), with a dark outline and shadow so they read on light and dark backgrounds.
  • sheets.json lists frame count, cell size, columns, rows, fps, loop and additive flags for every sheet.
  • Download size: CrispJuice_Full.zip is 51 MB.

Formats and engines

  • Transparent PNG sprite sheets, sorted by category, usable in any engine.
  • sheets.json with frame count, cell size, columns, rows, fps and loop flag for every sheet.
  • A Godot 4 project you can open and play, with the addon in Godot/addons/crisp_vfx.

Works with: Godot 4 (4.3 or newer): copy the addon folder into your project and drag an effect scene into your level, or call CrispVfx.spawn(); each scene plays when it appears and removes itself when it finishes. Unity, GameMaker, Construct, Phaser, Defold and others: import a sheet and slice it as a grid using the cell size from sheets.json (Unity: Sprite Mode Multiple, Slice, Grid By Cell Size; GameMaker: Import Strip Image; Phaser: load.spritesheet with frameWidth and frameHeight), then play the frames at 30 fps. No Unity prefabs or animation clips are included.

Which engines does it work with?

Any engine that can slice a PNG sprite sheet into a grid. The pack includes a Godot 4 project (4.3 or newer) with a scene per effect; the README gives the slicing steps for Unity, GameMaker and Phaser.

30 frames per second. sheets.json gives the frame count, cell size, columns and rows for every sheet.
