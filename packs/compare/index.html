Crisp Cozy Starter: the Godot 4 template

A finished cozy first-person game for Godot 4 to open, play and make your own: farming, cooking, dialogue, inventory, day / night, save / load, menus and settings, with a PS1-style look. A complete Godot 4 project: 40 GDScript files (about 3500 lines, typed and commented), 83 ready-to-place prop scenes, the Crisp Cute UI theme in six colour palettes, 30 item icons, 94 sound files and 111 automated tests. $19.99 on itch.io or direct by card on Payhip; the free browser demo is the real project exported to the web.

  • First-person player: walk, sprint, jump, mouse and right-stick look, footsteps that change with the floor
  • Interaction: doors, beds, the stove, the mailbox, the cat, pickups and garden beds share one Interactable base class
  • Inventory and hotbar: 24 slots (8 on the hotbar), stacking, move / swap / merge with mouse or controller
  • Farming: plant, water, crops grow overnight through 3 model stages, harvest (turnips, wheat, pumpkins)
  • Cooking / crafting: data-driven recipes per station (4 recipes at the wood stove)
  • Dialogue: branching conversations as resources, with flags, item checks, gifts and typewriter text
  • Day / night and sleep: clock from 6 am to 2 am; the bed skips to morning and auto-saves
  • Save / load: one readable JSON file
  • Menus and settings: main menu, pause menu, volumes, mouse and stick speed, invert Y, fullscreen, PSX look on / off
  • Keyboard and controller support for every action and menu
  • PSX look: low-res render, vertex snapping, affine textures, 15-bit colour and dither

Works with: Godot 4.4 or newer, standard or .NET build (pure GDScript). Built and tested in Godot 4.7.2.

Questions

Is this made with AI?

Yes. Claude (an AI assistant) made every pack, by writing the code that makes each asset: the sounds are synthesised from scratch, the graphics are drawn shape by shape and the 3D props are modelled and textured by code. No AI image, audio or 3D generators, no recorded samples, no scans and no third-party art. Every pack is opened and tested in real engines before it goes on sale. The store pages on itch.io carry the same AI disclosure.

Can I use these packs in a commercial game?

Yes. Each pack's LICENSE.txt allows use in any number of commercial and non-commercial projects (games, apps, websites, videos, streams, game jams), and you may edit and modify the files. You may not resell, redistribute or give away the files themselves, on their own or inside another asset pack. Most of the licence files sum it up as: put them in the things you make, just don't sell or share the pack. The music licence also asks you not to register the music with Content ID or any other fingerprinting service.

Do I need to credit Anipixels?

No. Credit is appreciated but never required.

Which engines do they work with?

The sound and music packs are plain WAV and OGG files, so any engine that plays those works. The UI kits, icons and sprite-sheet VFX are PNG (and SVG where listed) for any engine, with ready-made Godot 4 resources; Unity gets plain sprites or textures, not prefabs. The 3D props load anywhere that reads glTF, FBX or OBJ. Three are made for one engine: Crisp Juice Particles is Godot 4 only, Crisp Cute VN is Ren'Py 8 only, and Crisp Cozy Starter is a Godot 4.4 or newer project. The table lists each pack.

Is there a free version?

Every pack has a free sampler on its free itch.io page, under the same licence: for example 20 of the 242 Crisp UI sounds. Most free pages also have a browser demo of the whole pack; Crisp PSX Cozy Cabin, Crisp Cute VN and Crisp Juice Particles have the sampler only. Crisp Cozy Starter has a free browser demo and no free download.

How is Crisp Cozy different from Crisp UI?

Crisp UI is clean and neutral: clicks, glass, bright chimes. Crisp Cozy is warm and rounded: wood, kalimba, bubbles, paper, and it covers whole cozy games, not only menus. Measured across both packs, the average spectral centre of Crisp Cozy is about 1090 Hz against 1910 Hz for Crisp UI. The interface folder names match, so you can mix them.

What is the difference between Crisp Cozy and Crisp Cozy Life?

Since version 2.0 (26 October 2026), Crisp Cozy contains all 306 Crisp Cozy Life sounds unchanged, plus 1,057 more: the interface sounds, cozy home, cafe, dialogue voices, footsteps, weather, seasons, magic, critters, seaside, festival and ambience loops. Crisp Cozy Life on its own is the smaller gameplay set: tools, kitchen, workshop, pond, animals, footsteps.

Crisp Juice or Crisp Juice Particles?

Crisp Juice is 149 pre-drawn sprite-sheet effects (transparent PNG) that work in any engine, with Godot 4 scenes included. Crisp Juice Particles is 41 real-time effects made of Godot 4 particle scenes and shaders, each in 2D and 3D, so it works in Godot 4 only.
