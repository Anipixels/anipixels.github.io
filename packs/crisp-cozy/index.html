Crisp Cozy: 261 cozy UI sound effects

Crisp Cozy is a pack of 261 soft, cute user-interface sound effects for cozy games: farming and life sims, cat cafes, visual novels, casual puzzle and idle games. Every sound comes as WAV and OGG, is loudness-matched and is royalty-free for commercial use.

Details

  • 261 sounds in 10 folders (142 named sounds; 181 of the files belong to numbered sets, which are variations or tuned steps).
  • 2.7 minutes of audio in total; 179 of the 261 sounds are 0.6 s or shorter, and the longest jingle is 2.4 s.
  • Download size: 23 MB (one zip with both formats; 29 MB of WAV and 2.5 MB of OGG unzipped).
  • Tuned to C major pentatonic, so sounds layered together stay in tune.
  • Anything heard often has 2 to 5 variations; hovers and ticks are mixed quiet.
  • Error sounds are low marimba steps and soft pats, not buzzers.
  • Same folder names, loudness and key as Crisp UI, so the two packs can be mixed.

Formats and engines

  • WAV: 44.1 kHz, 16-bit, stereo (for editing and highest quality).
  • OGG Vorbis: small files, ready for Godot, Unity, GameMaker and web.
  • Every sound is loudness-matched, starts on its first sample (no dead air) and peaks at or below -1 dBFS.

Works with: Godot, Unity, Unreal, GameMaker, Construct, RPG Maker, Ren'Py, Phaser and other web frameworks: anything that plays WAV or OGG.

Can I use this in a commercial game?

Yes. One purchase covers unlimited commercial and non-commercial projects.

Do I have to credit you?

Which engines does it work with?

Any engine or tool that plays WAV or OGG files: Godot, Unity, Unreal, GameMaker, Construct, RPG Maker, Ren'Py, Phaser and other web frameworks.

How is Crisp Cozy different from Crisp UI?

Crisp UI is clean and neutral: clicks, glass, bright chimes. Crisp Cozy is warm and rounded: wood, kalimba, bubbles, paper, and it adds a Cozy Actions folder. No sound appears in both packs. Measured across both packs, the average spectral centre of Crisp Cozy is about 860 Hz against 1910 Hz for Crisp UI, and its sounds fade in more gently (median attack 10 ms against 2 ms). The folder names match, so you can swap one pack for the other or mix them.

How is Crisp Cozy different from Crisp Cozy Life?

Crisp Cozy is the user-interface pack: buttons, menus, notifications, rewards. Crisp Cozy Life is the gameplay pack: tools, kitchen, workshop, pond, animals, footsteps. No sound appears in both packs, and they share the same loudness and key, so they work side by side.

Was AI used?

Yes, for the code. Every sound is synthesised from scratch by code that was written with Claude (an AI assistant). No recorded samples and no AI audio-generation models were used.

Can I try before buying?

Yes. The web demo on the free itch.io page plays all 261 sounds in your browser, and the free sampler has 20 of them to download. The 20 sampler sounds can also be played on this site's UI sound board.
