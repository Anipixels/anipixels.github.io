Interface sounds, everyday actions, character voices, footsteps, weather, seasons, magic, critters and 25 seamless stereo ambience loops. Every sound comes as WAV and OGG, is loudness-matched and is royalty-free for commercial use.

Details

  • Version 2.0 (26 October 2026): the pack grew from 261 to 1,363 sounds, a free update for existing owners. Every v1.0 file is unchanged.
  • 1,363 sounds in 30 folders (574 named sounds; 398 of them come in several takes).
  • All 306 sounds of Crisp Cozy Life are included, unchanged, in their own folders (Farming, Cooking, Crafting, Fishing, Animals, Gathering, Home and Shop, Day Cycle).
  • 36 seamless loops: 25 stereo ambience loops of 12 to 14 s, plus cooking, fishing, purr, fire and rain loops.
  • 21 minutes of audio. One-shots are loudness-matched, start on their first sample and peak at or below -1 dBFS.
  • Downloads: everything in one 193 MB zip, or OGG only (17 MB), or WAV in 4 parts (227 MB of WAV and 20 MB of OGG unzipped).
  • Godot 4 sound bank included: one AudioStreamRandomizer per sound and a one-line play() autoload. Unity notes are included too.
  • Tuned to C major pentatonic, so sounds layered together stay in tune. Footsteps come in 6 takes, voices in 5 syllables; hovers and ticks are mixed quiet.
  • The interface folders use the same names, loudness and key as Crisp UI, so the two packs can be mixed.

Formats and engines

  • WAV: 44.1 kHz, 16-bit, stereo (for editing and highest quality).
  • OGG Vorbis: small files, ready for Godot, Unity, GameMaker and web.
  • Every sound is loudness-matched, starts on its first sample (no dead air) and peaks at or below -1 dBFS.

Works with: Godot (sound bank included), Unity, Unreal, GameMaker, Construct, RPG Maker, Ren'Py, Phaser and other web frameworks: anything that plays WAV or OGG.

Questions

Can I use this in a commercial game?

Yes. One purchase covers unlimited commercial and non-commercial projects.

Do I have to credit you?

No. A credit to Anipixels is welcome but not required.

Which engines does it work with?

Any engine or tool that plays WAV or OGG files: Godot (sound bank included), Unity, Unreal, GameMaker, Construct, RPG Maker, Ren'Py, Phaser and other web frameworks.

How is Crisp Cozy different from Crisp UI?

Crisp UI is clean and neutral: clicks, glass, bright chimes. Crisp Cozy is warm and rounded: wood, kalimba, bubbles, paper, and it covers whole cozy games, not only menus. Measured across both packs, the average spectral centre of Crisp Cozy is about 1090 Hz against 1910 Hz for Crisp UI. The interface folder names match, so you can mix them.

I own Crisp Cozy Life. What is different?

Since version 2.0 (26 October 2026), Crisp Cozy contains all 306 Crisp Cozy Life sounds unchanged, plus 1,057 more: the interface sounds, cozy home, cafe, dialogue voices, footsteps, weather, seasons, magic, critters, seaside, festival and ambience loops. Crisp Cozy Life on its own is the smaller gameplay set: tools, kitchen, workshop, pond, animals, footsteps.

Was AI used?

Yes, for the code. Every sound is synthesised from scratch by code that was written with Claude (an AI assistant). No recorded samples and no AI audio-generation models were used.

Yes. The web demo on the free itch.io page plays all 1,363 sounds in your browser (search, filter by folder, every take), and the free sampler has 20 of them to download. The 20 sampler sounds can also be played on this site's UI sound board.
