How it works

  • A 9-slice piece is cut by four border lines into nine regions. The four corners are drawn at their own size, the edges stretch in one direction and the middle stretches in both.
  • This page draws each piece with CSS border-image, using the slice_1x and slice_2x values from the sampler's sheets.json unchanged. The same four numbers go into the nine-slice or nine-patch settings of Godot, Unity, GameMaker, Construct or Phaser.
  • A few Crisp Cute pieces (stitches, cloud bumps) have middles made to repeat: sheets.json marks them with tile_middle, and this page tiles those middles with border-image-repeat: round instead of stretching them.
  • Pieces marked 3-slice have no top or bottom border: they stretch sideways only and keep their height.
  • Fixed-size pieces (icons, check boxes, knobs, prompts) have no borders and are shown at their own size.
  • Crisp Pixel is pixel art: its pieces are drawn from the 1x PNGs and enlarged by whole numbers with image-rendering: pixelated, so no pixel is smoothed. Do the same in your engine: whole-number scales with texture filtering off.
  • The text label on buttons sits inside the piece's content_padding_1x from sheets.json. The label is drawn in your browser's own font; Crisp Cute ships the free Fredoka font, the other kits include no font.
  • Keyboard: focus the round handle and use the arrow keys to resize (hold Shift for bigger steps), or use the width and height sliders.
