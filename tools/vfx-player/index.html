How to use it

  • Pick an effect above. It plays in the middle of the stage; click or tap anywhere on the stage to spawn more copies.
  • Drag the frame slider to step through the frames one by one. Speed changes the playback rate from 1 to 60 frames per second.
  • Additive blending adds the effect's colours to the background, which suits glows and sparkles on dark backgrounds. The player starts each sheet with the blend mode its sheets.json entry asks for.
  • Keyboard: K plays or pauses, Left and Right change effect, and Enter on the stage spawns a copy.
  • In your own engine, slice the sheet as a grid with the cell size shown and play the frames left to right, top to bottom. The code box shows the frame arithmetic.
