How to use it

  • Click or tap a sound to play it. Click it again to restart it.
  • Keyboard: Tab to a sound and press Enter or Space to play it; the arrow keys move between sounds; C copies the focused sound's file name; / jumps to the search box; Esc clears the search or stops all sounds.
  • The COPY button next to each sound copies its file name (for example click_soft_01.ogg), which is the name the file has inside the sampler zip.
  • To use a sound in your game, download that pack's free sampler from its itch.io page. It contains every sound here as WAV and OGG.

About the sounds

These are the 89 sounds of the five free samplers: 20 from Crisp UI, 20 from Crisp Puzzle, 9 from Crisp Jingles, 20 from Crisp Cards, 20 from Crisp Cozy. The full packs have 753 sounds between them and are sold on itch.io; they are not on this site.

The sampler files are WAV (44.1 kHz, 16-bit, stereo) and OGG Vorbis. This page plays the sampler's OGG files unchanged. For browsers that cannot play OGG Vorbis there is an MP3 copy of each sound, encoded from the sampler's WAV.
