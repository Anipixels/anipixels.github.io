How these are made

Claude (an AI assistant) made every pack, by writing the code that makes each asset: the sounds are synthesised from scratch, the graphics are drawn shape by shape and the 3D props are modelled and textured by code. No AI image, audio or 3D generators, no recorded samples, no scans and no third-party art. Every pack is opened and tested in real engines before it goes on sale.

Before a pack goes on sale it is imported and tested in a real engine, and its images and sounds are checked at full size. Each pack has a free sampler, so you can try it in your own project first. The store pages on itch.io carry the same AI disclosure.
